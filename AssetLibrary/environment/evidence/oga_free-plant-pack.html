Preview: 

i made a free plant pack with 13 different plants, textures are mine or from duions CC0 texture archive

i made variation for some plants and used cycles for preview rendering (simple shaders cause these darker areas in the render)

i tried it to make it as low poly as possible 

polycounts from plant 1 to 13, seen from second preview image

1 -  a) 172 (verts), 185 (tris) , b) 213 (verts) / 220 (tris)

2 -  112 / 140

3 - 229 / 276

4 - 382 / 290

6 - 30 / 28

7 -  30 / 24

8 - a) 48 / 48,  b) 96 / 96, c) 192 / 192

9 - a) 64 / 48, b) 112 / 84

10 -  138 / 144

11 - a) 186 / 176, b) 162 / 152

12 - a) 688 / 664, b) 724 / 700
