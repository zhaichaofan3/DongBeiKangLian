All editing and game integration described above was performed by this project. The original authors do not imply endorsement of the game.

Original archives, source pages, source license files, download URLs and SHA-256 hashes are preserved in the development workspace at `AssetLibrary/audio/manifest.json`. Prepared audio mappings are in `AssetLibrary/audio/ready_to_import.json`. The original three game derivatives are documented in `AssetLibrary/audio/game_integration.json`; the three 0.4 additions, with hashes and processing details, are documented in `docs/v04_audio_sources.json`. The project-wide attribution file is `GodotProject/ASSET_SOURCES.md`.

Other firearm, reload and hit sounds generated by `scripts/audio_factory.gd` are project-created. Unused audio in the download library is not represented as integrated content.


## 用户音频 · 2026-10-15

`opening_history_20261015.mp3` 为用户提供的《10月15日.mp3》原样副本，完整用于历史序章，含旁白及配乐。`global_war_music.ogg` 来自用户提供的《战争激昂的背景音乐.aac》，转为 Vorbis 并加首尾淡化，用于菜单、任务序幕及游戏全局，历史段停播。作者和授权名称未随文件提供，不标注为 CC0。原件、加工参数和 SHA-256 保存在 `AssetLibrary/audio/user_20261015/manifest.json`；实际混音听审待完成。

`user_dialogue_20261015/`包含用户新提供的8句姜岚录音，保持MP3原始声音，不变速、不重新合成。原始9份文件、两版木棚台词及逐文件校验保存在`AssetLibrary/audio/dialogue_20261015/`，此前选用的木棚(2)版本现已转为备用。未提供录音的对白保留字幕并静音；旧合成语音仅保留历史素材，不再用于当前游戏。

后续B5-L06、B6-L01按用户新指令替换为Downloads中的新版，两份新原件保存在`AssetLibrary/audio/dialogue_retake_20261015/`；旧原件保留。当前8句的最终选用与SHA-256见`user_dialogue_20261015/manifest.json`。
