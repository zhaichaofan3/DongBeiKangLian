MP Audio Solutions & Media

Professional Music, Sound Design & Interactive Audio Services

From original music and cinematic sound design to complete game audio production, MP Audio Solutions & Media provides creative and technical audio services for games, film, animation, interactive entertainment, and digital media.

Led by Matthew Pablo, an award-recognized Audio Director, Sound Designer, and Composer with over 14 years of professional experience, MP Audio Solutions brings production expertise across independent and major studio projects, including work involving Pokémon, Marvel, Disney, MLB, deadmau5, and Thick As Thieves.

Whether you're an independent developer looking for an original soundtrack, a filmmaker seeking a unique musical identity, or a studio needing experienced audio direction and technical implementation, services can be tailored to your project's creative goals, production requirements, and budget.

Music Composition & Production

Original music developed specifically for your project, from individual tracks to complete soundtracks.

Services include:

  • Original music composition for games, film, animation, trailers, and digital media

  • Full soundtrack production, arrangement, orchestration, recording, and mixing

  • Interactive and adaptive music systems for video games

  • Cinematic scoring, character themes, combat music, and environmental soundscapes

  • Electronic, orchestral, rock, metal, jazz, pop, chiptune, and hybrid musical styles

  • Music editing, looping, alternate arrangements, stems, and transitions

  • Soundtrack preparation for commercial release and digital distribution

  • Music supervision, licensing, and production consulting

Every project has its own musical identity. Original scores are developed to complement the creative direction, emotional storytelling, and experience of the audience.

Sound Design & Audio Post-Production

Creative sound design and professional audio production for interactive and linear media.

Services include:

  • Original sound effects and custom sound design

  • Gameplay, environmental, UI/UX, and cinematic audio

  • Foley, field recording, and custom sound library development

  • Dialogue editing, cleanup, restoration, and mixing

  • Voice-over production, recording direction, and asset preparation

  • Trailer, promotional, and cinematic sound design

  • Audio editing, mixing, and final delivery

  • Sonic branding and product audio identity

From subtle interface feedback to complex interactive soundscapes, sound design is developed with attention to storytelling, clarity, and the intended audience experience.

Game Audio Direction & Technical Implementation

Complete audio development services for game studios, including creative leadership, implementation, and production support.

With extensive experience in Unreal Engine, Unity, Wwise, and FMOD, MP Audio Solutions can support projects from early prototypes through production, launch, and live operations.

Services include:

  • Audio direction, creative planning, and audio identity development

  • Wwise and FMOD integration with Unreal Engine and Unity

  • Interactive music architecture and implementation

  • Gameplay audio systems, spatial audio, and environmental acoustics

  • Audio pipeline development, documentation, and technical design

  • Audio optimization, profiling, and performance management

  • Audio team leadership, mentorship, and production oversight

  • Vendor coordination, outsourcing, and external audio production

  • Audio prototyping, implementation support, and production consulting

Support is available for individual features, production milestones, or complete audio development across the life of a project.

Services for Filmmakers & Content Creators

Music and sound play an essential role in establishing tone, communicating emotion, and supporting visual storytelling.

MP Audio Solutions works with filmmakers, directors, producers, animators, and content creators to develop original scores, sound design, and finished audio for short films, feature projects, documentaries, trailers, animation, and online productions.

Existing compositions may also be available for licensing, depending on the intended usage and distribution requirements.

Services for Game Developers

Great game audio does more than accompany gameplay. It communicates information, reinforces player actions, builds atmosphere, and helps create memorable experiences.

MP Audio Solutions provides both creative and technical support for game developers, whether you need a handful of sound effects, an adaptive music system, or a complete audio production partner.

Original music is generally recommended for projects seeking a distinct creative identity, although existing tracks may be licensed or adapted for specific production needs.

Project Rates & Engagement Options

Every project is different, and audio services can be scoped around your production requirements, schedule, and available budget.

Engagement options include:

  • Per-asset or per-track pricing: Individual music compositions, sound effects, and other deliverables

  • Project-based agreements: Complete soundtracks, audio packages, or defined production milestones

  • Hourly or day rates: Technical implementation, consulting, sound design, and production support

  • Retainer or ongoing contracts: Continued development support, audio direction, and production services

  • Full-time or embedded engagements: Dedicated audio leadership or hands-on development

  • Licensing and revenue-sharing arrangements: Considered selectively depending on the project and terms

Independent developers and smaller creative teams are welcome to reach out. I am happy to discuss practical options that balance production quality, project scope, and budget.

Please contact me for a custom quote or to discuss your project's audio needs.

Existing Music & Licensing

Selected music available through my website may be considered for licensing in games, films, videos, streams, and other media.

Depending on the track and project, I may also be able to provide:

  • Custom edits and alternate arrangements

  • Seamless loops and shorter musical cues

  • Instrumental versions, stems, and alternate mixes

  • Adaptations for interactive playback

  • Custom compositions inspired by an existing musical direction

Please contact me before using or distributing music from my website. Publicly accessible music is not automatically royalty-free or cleared for commercial use.

Licensing permissions, fees, distribution rights, and attribution requirements will be established based on the intended use.
